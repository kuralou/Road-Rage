Sign Position Finder

Adjust the sign position to find where it should be placed in the game:

wrongWaySign.position.x = 20
wrongWaySign.position.y = -0.5
wrongWaySign.position.z = -20
wrongWaySign.scale.set(8, 8, 1)
Replace this in main.ts:

wrongWaySign.position.set(20, -0.5, -20);
wrongWaySign.scale.set(8, 8, 1);
